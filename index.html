About me

I’m an engineer with an academic background in design. I care how something feels to use, and I have the technical skills to build almost anything myself.

I’ve been building my own things since I was 12: games and robots in high school, startups since. I studied Creative Technology at the University of Twente and VU Amsterdam, with a minor in AI and Advanced Software Engineering in Budapest. Since 2025 I’ve been building AI for clients at AdamI, and my own products on the side.

Outside of work, I play volleyball at my club, which is also where Vizr started, when a coach asked for footage of our next opponent. I like to cook for friends, surf when I get the chance, and travel whenever I can, preferably somewhere with mountains or a coast. Most of my best ideas come when I’m away from a screen.
