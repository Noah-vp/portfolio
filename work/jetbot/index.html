JETBOT

WhatUniversity project · Just Eat Takeaway My roleTeam project Year2024

Just Eat Takeaway’s IT department got a lot of questions, many of them simple. So we built a robot that stands in front of the IT desk and that you can just talk to, filtering out the questions that don’t need a person.

Voice to voice, in a physical body we designed, built and spray-painted ourselves, right when ChatGPT had just come out. We tested it with their team at their office.
