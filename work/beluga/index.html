Beluga

WhatAI platform · AdamI My roleEngineer, now project lead Year2026

Almost every client wanted the same thing on top of what we built for them: a chat window. On their data, with an email tool, whatever they needed.

Instead of building that again every time, we built Beluga: a platform where we plug in each client’s custom AI, and they get it as their own product, with their own branding, roles and environment. Clients use it every day, partly for their custom solution and partly for Beluga’s own features, like a memory layer.

I started as an engineer on it and grew into the project lead: sprint planning, reviews and maintenance, while still building features myself. Today it’s used by 4 companies and around 100 people, including Tibo Veen, who run their private document chatbot on it, and Push.
